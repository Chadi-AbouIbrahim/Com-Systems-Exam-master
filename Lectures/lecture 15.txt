Now we will detector circuits 
basically pick and choose form the signals

first we have Envelope detector
it extracts R(t) from a bandpass signal
also has a check condition if the signals is distorted

RC envelope detector: cool shit, check equation

Next we have product detector
Mixer followed by a lowpass filter
used to extract phase for AM or PM


Lastly FM slope detector
This is a very primitive FM demodulator.

Modern receivers use quadrature detectors or PLLs (PLL skipped for your exam).


✔ Non-coherent detectors

Do NOT need carrier phase reference.

✔ Coherent detectors

Require synchronized oscillator.
